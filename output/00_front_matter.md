Ascent Partners (www.ascent-partners.com) is an independent valuation advisory firm based in Hong Kong, with offices in
mainland China and Singapore. The firm specializes in valuing early-stage and high-growth companies in the Asia-Pacific
region.

Practice Focus

Ascent Partners provides valuation services for pre-revenue and early-stage companies across technology, life sciences,
fintech, and consumer sectors. The firm’s practice includes:

Pre-revenue and seed stage valuations

Series A through growth stage valuations

Fair market value determinations for employee stock option grants

Cross-border transaction valuations

The firm serves entrepreneurs, investors, and corporations primarily in Hong Kong, mainland China, Singapore, and the
broader Asia-Pacific region.

Regional Expertise

With offices in Hong Kong, mainland China, and Singapore, Ascent Partners has experience with:

IFRS and local regulatory requirements in Asia-Pacific jurisdictions

Cross-border transactions involving Asian and international investors

Startup ecosystems in Hong Kong, mainland China, and Southeast Asia

The team works with regional venture capital firms, corporate investors, and startups operating across multiple Asian
markets.

About This Book

This book represents Ascent Partners’ experience in startup valuation, providing practical frameworks and methodologies

How to Use This Book

For Students

This textbook is designed to be accessible to graduate students with a foundation in corporate finance and basic
statistics. Each chapter follows a consistent structure:

Opening Vignette: A real company story that illustrates the chapter’s key concepts

Learning Objectives: Clear goals for what you should be able to do after completing the chapter

Main Content: Theory, methods, and examples with detailed explanations

Real-World Examples: Detailed case studies of actual companies

Key Takeaways: Summary of the most important points

Exercises: Problems ranging from basic to advanced, plus discussion questions and research projects

We recommend: - Read the opening vignette and learning objectives first to understand the chapter’s purpose - Work
through the examples step-by-step, replicating the calculations yourself - Attempt the exercises before looking at the
solutions - Build your own models to reinforce understanding - Participate in class discussions using the discussion
questions

For Instructors

This book provides comprehensive coverage suitable for a full semester course in startup valuation.

The book is designed for flexibility. A typical 14-week semester course might cover: - Weeks 1-2: Chapters 1-2
(Foundations) - Weeks 3-5: Chapters 3-5 (Methods) - Weeks 6-8: Chapters 6-8 (Cases, Regulation, Legal) - Weeks 9-10:
Chapters 9-10 (Pitfalls, Tools) - Weeks 11-13: Selected topics from Chapters 11-15 - Week 14: Student presentations or
final exam

For Practitioners

If you’re using this book as a professional reference, you may want to focus on: - Chapter 1: Understanding the
limitations of traditional methods - Chapter 3: Quick reference for core valuation methods - Chapter 6: Real-world case
studies - Chapter 7: Regulatory compliance requirements - Chapter 8: Legal precedents and litigation standards - Chapter
10: Practical tools and frameworks - Chapter 11: Industry-specific frameworks for your sector - Appendices: Quick
reference formulas and checklists
