# Appendix B: Regulatory Compliance Checklists

## Overview

### IFRS 13 Fair Value Measurement Checklist

☐ Identify the asset or liability to be measured

☐ Determine the principal (or most advantageous) market

☐ Determine the valuation premise (in-use vs. in-exchange)

☐ Determine appropriate valuation technique(s)

☐ Obtain inputs for valuation technique

☐ Classify inputs into Level 1, 2, or 3 hierarchy

☐ For Level 3: Document unobservable inputs and assumptions

☐ Perform sensitivity analysis on key assumptions

☐ Prepare required disclosures

☐ Obtain independent valuation (if material)

☐ Document valuation process and rationale

### ASC 820 Fair Value Measurement Checklist

☐ Define the unit of account

☐ Identify the principal market

☐ Determine highest and best use

☐ Select valuation technique (market, income, cost approach)

☐ Gather market participant assumptions

☐ Classify inputs (Level 1, 2, or 3)

☐ For Level 3: Document significant unobservable inputs

☐ Perform sensitivity analysis

☐ Reconcile Level 3 measurements (beginning to ending balance)

☐ Disclose valuation techniques and inputs

☐ Disclose transfers between levels

☐ Obtain audit review

### IRS Section 409A Valuation Checklist

☐ Engage qualified independent appraiser (for safe harbor)

☐ Obtain company financial statements

☐ Review capitalization table

☐ Analyze recent financing rounds or offers

☐ Assess company stage and development milestones

☐ Evaluate market conditions and comparables

☐ Calculate preferred stock value

☐ Apply OPM or PWERM for common stock discount

☐ Document all assumptions and methodologies

☐ Prepare formal valuation report

☐ Obtain board approval

☐ Update valuation every 12 months or upon material event

☐ Maintain valuation documentation for audit
