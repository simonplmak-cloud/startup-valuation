# Appendix D: Data Sources and Databases

## Overview

### Market Data

PitchBook: VC/PE deals, valuations, fund performance

CB Insights: Startup funding, unicorns, market intelligence

Crunchbase: Company profiles, funding rounds, investors

Capital IQ: Public company financials, M&A transactions

Bloomberg Terminal: Market data, company financials, news

### Valuation Multiples

SaaS Capital Index: SaaS company multiples and metrics

FactSet: Industry multiples, comparable companies

Refinitiv: M&A multiples, precedent transactions

Preqin: Private equity and VC benchmarks

### Industry-Specific

BIO: Biotech industry data, clinical trial success rates

Evaluate Pharma: Pharmaceutical market forecasts

App Annie: Mobile app downloads and revenue

Sensor Tower: App store analytics
