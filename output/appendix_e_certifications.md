# Glossary

409A Valuation: Independent appraisal of common stock fair market value required by IRS Section 409A for stock option
grants

Accredited Investor: Individual or entity meeting SEC wealth/income requirements to invest in private securities

Anti-Dilution Protection: Contractual provisions protecting investors from dilution in down rounds

ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue): Annualized value of recurring subscription revenue

ASC 820: U.S. GAAP accounting standard for fair value measurement

Berkus Method: Qualitative valuation method assigning up to $500K value to five key success factors

CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost): Total sales and marketing expense divided by new customers acquired

Cap Table: Capitalization table showing ownership percentages and equity distribution

Comparable Company Analysis: Valuation method using multiples from similar public companies

Convertible Note: Debt instrument that converts to equity at future financing round

Country Risk Premium: Additional return required for investing in emerging markets

DCF (Discounted Cash Flow): Valuation method based on present value of projected cash flows

Down Round: Financing round at lower valuation than previous round

EBITDA: Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization

Enterprise Value: Market value of equity plus net debt

Exit Multiple: Valuation multiple applied to terminal year financial metric in DCF

Fair Market Value (FMV): Price at which asset would trade between willing buyer and seller

First Chicago Method: Valuation using probability-weighted scenarios (best, base, worst case)

GMV (Gross Merchandise Value): Total value of transactions on marketplace platform

IFRS 13: International accounting standard for fair value measurement

Liquidation Preference: Amount preferred stockholders receive before common in exit

LTV (Lifetime Value): Total gross profit expected from customer over relationship

Monte Carlo Simulation: Valuation technique using thousands of random scenarios

NRR (Net Revenue Retention): Revenue retained from existing customers including expansion minus churn

OPM (Option Pricing Method): Method for valuing common stock as call option on enterprise value

Post-Money Valuation: Company valuation after investment

Pre-Money Valuation: Company valuation before investment

PWERM (Probability-Weighted Expected Return Method): Method valuing securities across multiple exit scenarios

Real Options: Valuation method treating strategic decisions as financial options

Risk Factor Summation: Valuation method adjusting base value for 12 risk factors

rNPV (Risk-Adjusted NPV): NPV calculation incorporating probability of success

SAFE (Simple Agreement for Future Equity): Investment instrument converting to equity at future round

Scorecard Method: Valuation method comparing startup to regional averages across seven factors

Take Rate: Percentage of GMV captured as revenue by marketplace

Term Sheet: Non-binding document outlining investment terms

Unicorn: Private company valued at $1 billion or more

Venture Capital Method: Valuation working backward from projected exit value and target return

WACC (Weighted Average Cost of Capital): Blended cost of debt and equity financing

Additional Key Terms:

Liquidation Preference: The order and amount that investors receive in a liquidation event before common shareholders.

Anti-Dilution Protection: Provisions that protect investors from dilution in down rounds by adjusting their conversion
price.

Drag-Along Rights: Rights that enable majority shareholders to force minority shareholders to join in the sale of a
company.

Tag-Along Rights: Rights that enable minority shareholders to join a transaction if majority shareholders sell their
stake.

Vesting Schedule: The timeline over which founders and employees earn their equity, typically 4 years with 1-year cliff.

409A Valuation: IRS-compliant valuation of common stock for stock option pricing purposes.

Participating Preferred: Preferred stock that receives liquidation preference AND participates in remaining proceeds
with common.

Fully Diluted: Share count including all outstanding shares, options, warrants, and convertible securities.

Pro Rata Rights: The right of investors to maintain their ownership percentage in future rounds.

Burn Rate: The rate at which a company spends its cash reserves, typically measured monthly.

Runway: The number of months a company can operate before running out of cash at current burn rate.

Cap Table: Capitalization table showing ownership stakes of all shareholders and option holders.

Down Round: A financing round at a lower valuation than the previous round.

Unicorn: A privately-held startup valued at $1 billion or more.

Decacorn: A privately-held startup valued at $10 billion or more.
